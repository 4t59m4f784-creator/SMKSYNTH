# Getting started

[Português](USER_GUIDE.md) · [English](USER_GUIDE.en.md)

Press PLAY on Pattern 1. Use KNOB BANK → Patterns and tap P1–P5 for the examples. P6–P16 start empty. Pattern changes during playback follow the sequencer’s scheduling.

## Persistent pad banks

| Bank | Controls |
|---|---|
| Performance / Favourites | Pads 1–4 select A–D; 5/6 toggle delay/reverb; 7/8 change preset; 9–16 recall favourites. Hold a favourite to store the selected part. Saving is manual; wait for completion. |
| Split / Layer | Two independent configurations of two ANALOG parts. Pads 8/16 select the playing row; editing the other row does not activate it. Both configurations and the active row are saved with the pattern. |
| Patterns / Scenes | Tap to load; hold to save. An occupied slot requires a second tap on the same pad within the confirmation window. There are 16 slots. |
| Drum Pads | Added to the cycle only when DRUM-X is selected. The eight upper pads play/record KICK, SNARE, CLAP, HAT CL, HAT OP, TOM, RIM and BELL with velocity. Lower pads are reserved and unlit. |

KNOB BANK cycles through Performance → Split/Layer → Patterns, adding Drum Pads with DRUM-X selected. PLAY/REC preserve your chosen bank. SEQ explicitly opens sequencer steps. Hold KNOB BANK to return to Performance.

Drum Pads are violet at rest and white on live or sequenced hits. Selecting ANALOG keeps this bank inactive. Selecting a drum kit does not automatically activate Drum Pads.

## Parts and controls

Use K6 to select a part. Press GLOBE until PART SETUP, then K1 selects ANALOG/DRUM-X; K2 selects the DRUM-X kit.

K1–K4 follow the current screen. K5 controls tempo on Performance; K6 selects part, K7 preset and K8 master. Fader Bank A offers macros. Bank B offers the part’s chorus/delay/reverb sends and master. Sends use pickup.

SEQ TRANSPOSE: K1 enables keyboard capture, K2 adjusts offset, K3 resets offset. Capture persists when leaving the screen. Performance K4 displays KEY TR / ON while enabled; turn left to disable capture. Disabling capture does not reset the offset. Layers retain their held-note commands.

Step recording, live recording and the arpeggiator account for physical keyboard and split/layer octave settings. Re-recording a step’s notes resets that step’s previous nudge; other steps keep their microtiming.

## Scenes and saving

Patterns store sequences, four sounds, mixer and keyboard configurations as scenes. Tempo is global; examples start at 90 BPM. Scene and favourite sounds are snapshots independent of the preset bank. No extra personal presets are installed.

SAVE on the panel, then export your bank. Hardware and simulator use separate memories. A simulator session is not a hardware bank backup.
